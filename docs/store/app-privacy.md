# App Store privacy — DRAFT

Confirm these answers against the final privacy policy and SDK inventory before submission.

## Data collected

- Contact information: name, email address and phone number.
- User content: job photos, notes, site instructions and other uploaded files.
- Identifiers: internal user ID and tenant/business ID.

All listed data is used for app functionality, is linked to the user or their business account, and is not used for tracking. The app has no third-party advertising. Supabase is the backend processor; verify any future analytics, crash-reporting or push provider before keeping the “no tracking” answer.

## Age rating draft

No unrestricted web access, gambling, contests, simulated gambling, sexual content, profanity, horror, violence, alcohol/tobacco/drugs, medical treatment information, or loot boxes. User-generated work notes and photos are limited to members of the same invited business. Confirm answers in App Store Connect.

## Account deletion open question

Accounts are invite-only and users cannot create their own account. Apple’s final interpretation must be confirmed during review preparation. The smallest operational option is a deletion-request page linked from the app, backed by a platform-admin procedure that deletes or anonymises the user in accordance with retention obligations.
