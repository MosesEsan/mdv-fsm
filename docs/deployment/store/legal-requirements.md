# Privacy policy and terms requirements

Have qualified counsel approve the final wording. It should cover:

- controller/processor roles for the platform, each client business, Supabase and the email provider;
- GDPR lawful bases, data-subject rights, contact channel and complaint route;
- EU hosting in Ireland (`eu-west-1`) and any subprocessors or international transfers;
- job photos, notes, contact details, location/site information and uploaded files;
- access controls, security responsibilities and incident handling;
- retention periods, backups and deletion/anonymisation;
- data export and handover when a client leaves;
- acceptable use, account security, service availability and liability;
- account deletion requests and legal/contractual retention exceptions;
- policy effective date, versioning and notice of material changes.
